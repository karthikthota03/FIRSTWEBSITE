Research..!

Stock market research is the process of studying companies and market conditions before investing money. It helps beginners understand how a company operates, earns profits, and grows over time. Research involves analyzing financial performance, business fundamentals, and industry trends. Investors also look at economic news and market movements that affect stock prices. Proper research helps reduce risk, make informed decisions, and build long-term investing confidence.
